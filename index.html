Corrections and checked facts

  • Agencies: WA resource regulation sits with the Department of Mines, Petroleum and Exploration (DMPE), formerly DMIRS/DEMIRS, from 1 Jul 2025 (start date still to check against the Gazette). Qld: Department of Natural Resources and Mines, Manufacturing and Regional and Rural Development, from 1 Nov 2024. NT: Department of Mining and Energy, from Sep 2024. NSW: NSW Resources, a division of the Department of Primary Industries and Regional Development (reported from 1 Jul 2024). Only the WA change dates from 1 Jul 2025.
  • Dates: China removed barley duties from 5 Aug 2023 and wine duties from 29 Mar 2024. CMPTI passed Parliament on 11 Feb 2025 (Royal Assent 14 Feb 2025) and applies to production from 1 Jul 2027 to 30 Jun 2040. The EPBC reform Act received Royal Assent on 1 Dec 2025.
  • Entities: BHP's Chinese name is 必和必拓 (not 比利顿). Arafura has been Arafura Rare Earths since 2022. Northern Minerals and Arafura are on a watch list outside the ASX 200 Rotation scope. Whitehaven is GICS Energy, measured against the ASX 200 Energy index.
  • Time-sensitive: China's suspension of its Oct 2025 rare earth export controls runs to 10 Jan 2027 (MOFCOM/GACC Ann. 58 of 2026). BHP told the ASX on 22 Apr 2026 that its CMRG negotiations had concluded. The second set of Northern Minerals disposal orders was registered on 18 May 2026 and commenced on 18 Jun 2026.

How the data gets here

A scheduled GitHub Actions job runs the Python fetchers in fetchers/ on weekdays. It writes JSON into data/ and commits it, and Cloudflare Pages redeploys. The browser only reads same-origin files: no third-party calls, no API keys and no tracking.

Sources: ASX announcement lists (time, type, price-sensitive flag and a link; headlines are withheld because ASX's site terms bar republishing without consent); wa.gov.au DMPE announcements; WA Mining Tenements DMIRS-003 (© State of WA, CC BY 4.0). Tenements held only by individuals are left out.
